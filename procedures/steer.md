**When to run**: when capacity needs a direction — a prior initiative concluded, or replenishment is about to run with no live initiative to draw its goal from — or on a slow cadence to re-check that the live bet is still the right bet. Steering is slower than replenishment: one pass feeds several replenishment cycles.

**The concept.** Steering is not top-down. It is reading **all three altitudes** — the strategic bets, the recent increments, and the raw demand in the pool — and deciding where the next unit of capacity goes. Strategically aligned work and regular demand (bug fixes, tactical friction, ideas born at source) compete for the same capacity, and both have full standing: some passes the honest call is "advance the bet," others it is "the pool is accumulating friction — this cycle serves hygiene." That demand-mix judgment IS the steering act. Its output is one **initiative**: an outcome sought, not a work list — the work list is the plan's job, one level down, and keeping them separate is what lets a plan be judged against the initiative rather than against itself.

**Propose, don't revise.** Steering reads the strategic records but never flips them mid-pass: a strategy moving hypothesis → evidenced (or → refuted), a bet being retired — these are proposals in the report, disposed between beats, not during one. The one record steering writes is the initiative it produces.

## The pass

1. **Evaluate the strategic level.** Read the portfolio view (`views/PORTFOLIO.md`) and the strategy records behind it — statuses are the confidence axis; where the weight sits is the state of the bets. Read the initiatives at the initiative type's declared path (no roll-up view projects them yet; an empty or absent directory means no initiative has ever been minted — a normal cold start, and this pass mints the first). If one is live (at its committed status) and its outcome has not arrived or died, the steering call may simply be **continue** — report that and stop; do not mint a rival initiative while one is live (strategic WIP is the scarcest limit there is). Note where accumulated evidence bears on a strategy's status — that becomes a proposal in step 5.

2. **Evaluate the coordination layer — the most recent increments.** Read the settled and committed plan records (at the plan type's declared path), the recent deliveries (the done column in `views/BOARD.md` and the git log), and the metrics view if the board writes one. What did the last tranches buy against their goals? What did settlement book onto positions — and did any delivery ship without its outcome arriving (delivered ≠ fit)? A fitness gap at this layer is steering signal: the last bet may need another push, or a different instrument, before any new direction deserves capacity.

3. **Read the pool's demand signal.** Orient from the read-models, not the raw store: `views/POOL.md` (the full pool, one line per card with age and refs) and the upstream region of `views/METRICS.md` (depth, aging percentiles, arrival rate) if the board writes it. Not a triage — triage is replenishment's job, run just-in-time at the pull moment — but at a deep pool this is still a real fold, not a glance: cluster by territory, read both the fresh end and the aging tail. The question is what demand is saying: what is accumulating, what clusters, what ages. A cluster of friction cards from the same territory is bottom-up strategy arriving at source; a swelling of tactical debt is a hygiene claim on the next cycles. Weigh what the pool is saying against what the bets are asking.

4. **Produce the initiative — outcome-focused, materialized.** One initiative per pass. State it as an outcome: what should be true when it concludes, and **how we would know** — an observable, checkable arrival signal, honest enough that "it did not arrive" is a reportable conclusion. Never a work list, never a card enumeration — and no work-unit sizing either: the outcome bounds the initiative, and scope problems are downstream's business to surface (replenishment compiles the plans, plan review judges them against the outcome; a bet that keeps spawning tranches without its arrival signal moving is how "too big" actually shows up). The one sizing judgment steering owes is categorical: an outcome that is really a standing direction is a strategy wearing the wrong type — write the strategy record instead. Slug it as an **imperative verb phrase** — the bet as a directive (`counter-append-drift`, `orient-options-pool`), never a territory noun: nouns name capabilities, and a slug that won't take a leading verb is the categorical tell that the "initiative" is really a strategy or capability wearing the wrong type. Materialize it (`kanbento note --type initiative --slug <slug> -F <file>` — the record is stamped at the type's first status, proposed, automatically), then ref what it advances (`kanbento link initiative:<slug> advances <strategy-or-position>`) — an initiative feeding no position should say why it deserves capacity anyway (hygiene is a valid why; state it).

5. **Disposition gate — present, then halt.** Show the initiative and the strategy-status proposals from step 1, then stop: with no human present, the pass ends here with the record at proposed — report and terminate, never loop or self-dispose. The go/no-go is the initiative's **status flip** (proposed → committed, or → declined — a no-go is decision history, kept), and an edited record is a verdict — the surviving text is the authorized outcome. Once committed, the initiative is what replenishment cycles draw their goals from (`kanbento do replenish goal="…"`, the goal derived from — and the plan linked `advances` to — the initiative) until the outcome arrives or the bet is called off. Conclusion is a later, separate act: when the arrival signal is checked, flip to concluded with the outcome honestly stated on the record — arrived, partially, or not.

6. **Harvest + report.** Friction met while steering → `kanbento capture`; a contested steering call → `kanbento cases retain` (reuse a category before minting one). Report: the state of the bets in three lines, what the increments and the pool said, the initiative (or the continue call), and the proposals awaiting a human.
